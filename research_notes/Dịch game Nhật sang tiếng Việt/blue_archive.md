# Blue Archive (ブルーアーカイブ) JP->VI translation profile seed

Research date: 2026-10-24. Sources are mostly English and Japanese wikis and store pages. I found almost no Vietnamese-language material on terminology or pronouns, so the Vietnamese conventions below are mostly inferences and are labeled that way.

## 1. How 先生 (Sensei) is addressed, and how EN and VI handle Sensei and student<->Sensei pronouns

### Takeaway
In the Japanese, students call the player 先生 and usually drop any pronoun. The English text keeps "Sensei" every time. In-game the protagonist is unvoiced and has no gender. There is no official Vietnamese localization, and the few Vietnamese fan works I found leave "Sensei" untranslated. I found no documented Vietnamese convention for student<->Sensei pronouns, so the profile has to set one.

### Cited Findings
- JP term: the player character is simply 先生 (listed among the setting terms next to 学園都市「キヴォトス」 and 連邦捜査部「シャーレ」) — [ja.wikipedia ブルーアーカイブ](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ブルーアーカイブ)
- The player is "Sensei", a teacher. Wikipedia's character table says the protagonist is unvoiced and ungendered in-game — [en.wikipedia Blue Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Archive)
- Wikipedia describes the player as a Sensei summoned from outside the academic city-state of Kivotos — [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Archive)
- Line tables show the English keeps 先生 as "Sensei", e.g. Hoshino's 「よく来たね、先生。」 becomes "Welcome, Sensei…" (Blue Archive © NEXON Games Co., Ltd.; lines via bluearchive.wiki). Aris's lines do the same. Japanese lines usually leave out a second-person pronoun and say 先生 instead — [bluearchive.wiki Hoshino/audio](https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Hoshino/audio); [bluearchive.wiki Arisu/audio](https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Arisu/audio)
- No official Vietnamese: the Vietnamese Steam page lists "Tiếng Việt | Không được hỗ trợ" for interface, audio and subtitles — [Steam (vi)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3557620/Blue_Archive/?l=vietnamese)
- Vietnamese fan prose keeps "Sensei". A Vietnamese Wattpad translation of a Korean BA fanfic writes 'Sensei' in quotes and uses the male "anh/anh ấy" in narration (e.g. "Nhưng anh không đổ lỗi cho học sinh."). Its source is Korean author Bisolti/A2 on Novelpia — [Wattpad: Blue Archive: Nơi mọi căm hận bắt đầu](https://www.wattpad.com/story/392461954-blue-archive-n%C6%A1i-mo%CC%A3i-c%C4%83m-h%C3%A2%CC%A3n-b%C4%83%CC%81t-%C4%91%C3%A2%CC%80u-quy%C3%AA%CC%89n-1)
- Vietnamese video titles also keep "Sensei", e.g. TikTok "Cái Chết Của Sensei Blue Archive Vietsub" — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/c%C3%A1i-ch%E1%BA%BFt-c%E1%BB%A7a-sensei-blue-archive-vietsub)
- Vietnamese fan subtitles exist for the anime, e.g. "[S1] Tập 01 - Blue Archive The Animation [Vietsub]" on Bilibili.tv — [bilibili.tv](https://www.bilibili.tv/en/video/4795259739183104)
- Anime: *Blue Archive The Animation* ran 12 episodes from 2024-04-07 to 06-23 on TV Tokyo, BS11 and AT-X. It was made by Yostar Pictures and studio CANDYBOX and directed by 山岸大悟 (Daigo Yamagishi). Medialink licensed it for South and Southeast Asia — [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Archive); [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ブルーアーカイブ)

(Global)* 4-koma (about 159–169 chapters, depending on the site) and *Blue Archive: Cuộc Phiêu Lưu Của Câu Lạc Bộ Phát Triển Game* (18 chapters). Both are unofficial — [truyentranhmanhua (4-koma)](https://truyentranhmanhua.org/truyen-tranh/blue-archive-global); [truyentranhmanhua (GDD)](https://truyentranhmanhua.org/truyen-tranh/blue-archive-cuoc-phieu-luu-cua-cau-lac-bo-phat-trien-game); [TruyenQQ](https://truyenqq.com.vn/blue-archive-global); [NetTruyen](https://nettruyen.fit/truyen-tranh/blue-archive-global)
- Official spinoff manga include *Problem Solver 68 Business Diary* (Bushiroad; English edition by Kaiten Books) and *The Grand Adventure of the Game Development Department* (Square Enix) — [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Archive)

### Inferences
- Default rule: keep "Sensei" untranslated in all VI output. EN Global, fan prose and Vietnamese video titles all do this. It also avoids gendering the player with "thầy" or "cô", since the game itself is ungendered.
- Suggested pronouns, gender-neutral mode (default): students say **em** for themselves and call the player **Sensei** ("Em cảm ơn Sensei ạ"). Sensei uses **tôi** (neutral) for self and **em** or the student's name for students. Arona and Plana also use em/Sensei. Superiors or adults speaking to Sensei (GSC staff, Kaiser, Black Suit/黒服) use **tôi/ngài** or **tôi/Sensei**. Antagonists may use **ta/ngươi**.
- Optional "male Sensei" mode, for users who want it to match the anime and fanfic default: students **em** / **thầy** or Sensei; Sensei says **thầy** for self. The Wattpad "anh" narration shows Vietnamese fans often assume a male Sensei. Make this a profile toggle, not the default.
- Particles: students' polite です/ます speech toward Sensei maps to Vietnamese "ạ" or "nhé" endings. Casual characters (Hoshino, Mika, Momoi) drop "ạ".

### Gaps
- I found no Vietnamese fan glossary, Facebook group post or Discord-public page that documents pronoun conventions for BA. The Vietsub anime pronoun choices could not be read; the pages are video only.
- Whether Medialink/Ani-One provided official Vietnamese subtitles for the anime is unconfirmed.
- Vietnamese scanlation pages are image-based, so I could not extract their pronoun choices. Only the title rendering "Câu Lạc Bộ Phát Triển Game" was visible.

## 2. Key proper nouns: JP names, official EN names, VI renderings

### Takeaway
The core JP/EN pairs are well attested: キヴォトス/Kivotos, 連邦生徒会/General Student Council, 連邦捜査部シャーレ/SCHALE (Schale), シッテムの箱/Shittim Chest, アロナ/Arona, プラナ/Plana, and full school names such as アビドス高等学校/Abydos High School. Club names in EN are Global's localized forms (Foreclosure Task Force, Prefect Team, etc.). Vietnamese fans keep proper nouns in their romanized EN form and translate generic club words; the one attested example is "Câu Lạc Bộ Phát Triển Game".

Translate the generic part: 高等学校 -> "Trường cao trung" / "Học viện", 部 -> "CLB", 委員会 -> "Ủy ban", 生徒会 -> "Hội học sinh", 連邦生徒会 -> "Hội học sinh Liên bang" (EN "General Student Council").
- Suggested VI pairs (unverified, for review): 対策委員会 -> "Ủy ban Đối sách (Abydos)"; 風紀委員会 -> "Ủy ban Kỷ luật" (EN Prefect Team); 便利屋68 -> "Văn phòng Vạn năng 68" or keep "Problem Solver 68"; ゲーム開発部 -> "CLB Phát triển Game" (attested); 補習授業部 -> "CLB Học bù" (EN Make-Up Work Club); セミナー -> "Seminar" (Millennium's student council, kept); 万魔殿 -> "Vạn Ma Điện (Pandemonium Society)"; ティーパーティー -> "Tea Party" or "Hội Trà"; シスターフッド -> "Sisterhood"; 美食研究会 -> "Hội Nghiên cứu Ẩm thực"; 放課後スイーツ部 -> "CLB Đồ ngọt Sau giờ học".
- Because Vietnamese can read 漢字-derived Hán-Việt, kanji org names like 万魔殿, 玄龍門 or 百花繚乱 can get Hán-Việt renderings (Vạn Ma Điện, Huyền Long Môn, Bách Hoa Liêu Loạn). Many VI anime/gacha fans prefer this for a flavorful tone. Make it a style choice and apply it consistently.

### Gaps

## 3. Character name conventions and distinctive speech

### Takeaway
JP names are surname kanji plus a katakana given name (砂狼シロコ, 天童アリス, 早瀬ユウカ, 聖園ミカ). The UI and wikis show the given name only, with outfit variants in parentheses ("Hoshino (Swimsuit)", "Shiroko＊Terror"). Romanization differs by source for アリス: bluearchive.wiki uses "Arisu", Fandom "Alice", merch and pixiv "Aris". The English localization does not render quirks as fixed tokens. Hoshino's うへ～ varies ("Uhe~", "Uhee~", "Ugh."), her self-label おじさん becomes "old-timer like me", and Aris's RPG phrasing is translated literally into game jargon.

### Cited Findings
- Full names in JP: 砂狼シロコ, 天童アリス, 早瀬ユウカ, 聖園ミカ — [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ブルーアーカイブ)

### Inferences
- Name policy for VI: show the given name only, romanized in Global/wiki style with long vowels kept (Yuuka, Kikyou, Hoshino). Strip variant tags for glossary matching: "Hoshino (Swimsuit)" maps to Hoshino, "Shiroko＊Terror" maps to Shiroko (Terror). Map the katakana given name to the romaji, e.g. ホシノ -> Hoshino, シロコ -> Shiroko, ユウカ -> Yuuka, アリス -> Aris (or Arisu, configurable), ヒナ -> Hina, ヒフミ -> Hifumi, コハル -> Koharu, ミカ -> Mika. For アリス, pick one form ("Aris" is closest to how many EN players say it; "Arisu" matches bluearchive.wiki) and lock it.
- Honorific suffixes (〜ちゃん, 〜さん, 〜先輩, 〜様, 〜くん): keep "-senpai" as "senpai" or translate as "chị/tiền bối". Drop 〜さん. Turn 〜ちゃん into "bé X" or drop it. Profile toggle.
- Quirk carry-over (suggestions):
  - Aris: third-person name self-reference is natural in Vietnamese ("Aris vui lắm ạ!"), so keep it rather than switching to "em". Render RPG jargon with Vietnamese gamer terms: 勇者 -> "dũng giả" or "anh hùng", レベルアップ -> "Lên cấp!", HP stays "HP", 経験値 -> "điểm kinh nghiệm (EXP)", パンパカパーン -> "Tèn ten!".
  - Hoshino: keep the interjection as "Uhe~" (fans recognize it) or "Ưm hê~". Render おじさん as "ông chú này" or "lão già này" to keep the self-deprecating joke, and keep her drawn-out "~" endings ("nhỉ~", "mà~").
  - Shiroko: her terse ん becomes "Ừm." or "Ừ."; keep sentences short and flat.
  - Koharu, Hina, Mika, Hifumi: I found no sourced examples. Plausible approaches: Koharu's catchphrase scolding (anything lewd is forbidden and gets the death penalty) as a short indignant "…là không được! Tử hình!"; Hina formal and tired; Mika playful with "☆" and teasing; Hifumi timid with "a-ha-ha…" stutters.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the official Global in-game romanization for アリス ("Aris" vs "Arisu"), because the official site and client text were not checked. The sources conflict.
- No sourced line examples were found for Shiroko's ん, Koharu, Hina, Mika or Hifumi. Their bluearchive.wiki /audio pages (same format as Hoshino/audio) are the next place to look.
- Surnames for Hoshino (小鳥遊), Hina (空崎), Hifumi (阿慈谷) and Koharu (下江) were not confirmed in the ja.wikipedia portion read.

## 4. Glossaries, wikis and datasets usable as profile seeds

### Takeaway
There is no ready-made JP/EN/VI glossary. Public wikis give the names, schools and clubs (bluearchive.wiki, Wikipedia), and bluearchive.wiki /audio pages pair JP and EN voice lines. Game text is copyrighted by NEXON Games/Yostar, so use it for terminology only.

### Cited Findings
- Community dumps of the game's data files (localization tables, assets) exist; this project does not use them, and its profile is not built from game files.
- **deepghs/game_characters (HuggingFace)**: `bluearchive/pixiv_names.json`, a mapping of character name aliases to tags (useful for spelling variants) — [HuggingFace deepghs](https://huggingface.co/datasets/deepghs/game_characters/blob/fb6d9c6062e8d04ec70f474c6fd29aafcdf64518/bluearchive/pixiv_names.json)

The Characters page groups the roster by school — [bluearchive.wiki Hoshino/audio](https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Hoshino/audio); [bluearchive.wiki Characters](https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Characters)
- **Fandom Blue Archive Wiki** (bluearchive.fandom.com) has character, school and audio pages, but it uses different romanization ("Tendou Alice"). It blocked automated fetch (HTTP 402) during this research — [Fandom Tendou Alice](https://bluearchive.fandom.com/wiki/Tendou_Alice)

### Inferences
- Build the glossary from public wiki facts (character, school and club names on bluearchive.wiki and Wikipedia) and hand-author the VI column, starting from the proposals in section 2. Write the character-sheet speech notes in your own words after reading the bluearchive.wiki /audio pages.
- Use game text for terminology only; don't redistribute story text or voice lines. Game text is copyrighted by NEXON Games/Yostar.

### Gaps
- No license was found for bluearchive.wiki content or the Fandom wiki in what I read. Fandom is usually CC BY-SA, but I did not verify that here.
- No community JP->EN or JP->VI terminology spreadsheet was found.

## 5. Technical notes for screen-translating Blue Archive on PC

### Takeaway
PC versions run kernel-level anti-cheat: Nexon Game Security (NGS) on the Steam/Global build and XIGNCODE3 on the Yostar JP PC build. Hooking is therefore risky, and OCR of the game window is the safe default. The Global Steam build is not sold in Vietnam (geo-excluded), so Vietnamese players are likely on mobile, emulators or the JP PC client. The game's text languages are Kr/Jp/Th/Tw/En. JP text is available on the Steam client, and on the JP client as the base language.

### Cited Findings
- Windows release: worldwide on 2025-07-04 via Steam, excluding Benelux, Vietnam, Germany and Yostar-managed regions. Japan PC followed on 2026-01-20 — [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Archive); JP PC service start 2026年1月20日 — [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ブルーアーカイブ)
- When fetched, the Steam store page returned "This item is currently unavailable in your region" (fetcher region unknown) — [Steam app 3557620](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3557620/Blue_Archive/)
- Anti-cheat is Nexon Game Security for the Steam version and XIGNCODE3 for the Yostar version — [PCGamingWiki (search snippet)](https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Blue_Archive)
- GamingOnLinux lists Blue Archive as working on Proton, with NGS as the anti-cheat — [GamingOnLinux itemdb](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/itemdb/app/42337/blue-archive/)
- The Steam client offers Kr/Jp/Th/Tw/En text, as community translation projects state (not checked against the client).
- Hook precedent: a Russian Steam guide uses a modified LunaTranslator in HOOK mode on `BlueArchive.exe`, with source language English and code page UTF-8. It injects a ready-made translation rather than machine-translating live, and the setup is poorly documented. The page itself returned 403/429, so this comes from the search snippet only — [Steam Community guide "Русификатор Blue archive"](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3768501145)
- Developer: Nexon Games (MX Studio). Publishers: Yostar for JP/CN, Nexon worldwide. Release dates: JP 2021-02-04, Global 2021-11-08/09, CN 2023-08-03 — [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Archive); [ja.wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ブルーアーカイブ)

### Inferences
- Recommend OCR capture, not memory or text hooking. Under NGS or XIGNCODE3, injecting into `BlueArchive.exe` risks detection or a ban. No source confirms hooks are safe, and the one hook guide is undocumented. Capturing a window or region is non-invasive, and it works the same for the Steam build, the JP PC client and Android emulators (common in Vietnam).
- The Steam Global client offers JP text, so a JP->VI pipeline can run on it with the in-game language set to Japanese. EN text could also serve as a pivot (EN->VI), and is often cleaner for OCR. The profile could support both source languages.
- OCR layout to configure (from typical BA visual-novel scenes; verify in the client): a bottom dialogue box with the speaker name, often next to a smaller club/affiliation label, above the dialogue text. Sensei's choice options appear as separate buttons. Treat the name label as a separate OCR region and resolve it against the character glossary, so the name is never machine-translated.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: the exact textbox layout (whether a club label sits next to the name), whether JP story text uses furigana/ruby, and the font. These need a screenshot from the client.
- No source says whether NGS/XIGNCODE3 flag window-capture or OCR tools or overlay windows. That is believed to be safe but unverified.
- Not confirmed: whether the JP PC client (Yostar, 2026-01-20) can be bought or run from Vietnam, and whether a Nexon-launcher PC route exists for Vietnam.
